High Summer Temperatures Reduce Panel Efficiency

High Summer Temperatures Reduce Panel Efficiency

Posted by Acme Solar on 2026-06-19

Photovoltaic output falls slightly as cell temperature rises, so the hottest afternoons do not necessarily deliver the highest production despite the strong sunshine. Mounting height and airflow beneath the array affect how much heat is shed, which is one reason installation detail influences long-term yield. Production estimates that account for local temperature profiles give a more realistic picture than ones based on irradiance alone.
