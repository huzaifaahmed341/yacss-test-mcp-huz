Panel Recycling and End-of-Life Handling Draw More Attention

Panel Recycling and End-of-Life Handling Draw More Attention

Posted by Acme Solar on 2026-03-30

Solar panels have long operating lives, and the first large wave of installations is now approaching the end of its performance warranty period. That has increased interest in how panels are handled at end of life, including recovery of glass, aluminium framing and the semiconductor materials inside the cells. Recycling infrastructure continues to develop, and owners planning replacements should ask where retired modules will go.
