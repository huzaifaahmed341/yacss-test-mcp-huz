What a redirect map should contain before a migration

What a redirect map should contain before a migration

Posted by Jesper Nissen on 2026-07-11

The redirect map is the single artefact that decides whether a migration keeps its rankings. It should be built from a full crawl of the live site plus every URL that has earned a link or received organic traffic in the past year, because plenty of valuable pages are not linked from anywhere current. Each old URL maps to the closest equivalent, not to the homepage. Chains get flattened to a single hop. Then it is tested on staging before launch rather than discovered in production.
